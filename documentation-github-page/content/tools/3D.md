## Visuals in 3D!

Even as a hobbyist you can now use free tools to create and test hardware enclosures without bending metal, sawing 
wood and risking your fingers, or hurting yourself with a screwdriver.

### 3D

For a developer [OpenSCAD](https://openscad.org/) might be a perfect 3D tool, 
because you create your model by logical operations 
using a programming language instead of fighting with a 3D mouse-driven UI.
You can easily import and use plenty of free 3D models from [GrabCad](https://grabcad.com/) to create your product. 
Sample?

Just search for LCD 2004 and choose a model of your choice supporting STEP or STL. OpenSCAD supports STL, 
for STEP a little conversion step is required, for [example](https://anyconv.com/step-to-stl-converter/).
